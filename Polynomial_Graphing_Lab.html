💡 Need a hint?

Pick an x-value, substitute it into the equation, and calculate y. For a better curve, include points on both sides of important features such as the vertex or turning points.

📚 Graphing reminders

Quadratics have degree 2, cubics degree 3, and quartics degree 4. The degree is the maximum possible number of distinct real x-intercepts. Use a table of values and look for intercepts and turning points.

🧠 How is the score calculated?

70 points: accuracy of your plotted coordinates. 20 points: covering the suggested horizontal interval. 10 points: at least five different x-values. Incorrect points lower accuracy. The smooth blue curve is a visual guide only and is not independently graded.
