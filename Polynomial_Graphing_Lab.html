💡 Need a hint?

📚 How do I graph a polynomial?
  1. Choose x-values in the visible grid.
  2. Substitute each x-value into the equation to calculate y.
  3. Click the corresponding coordinate on the grid.
  4. Plot at least five points, including points on both sides of x = 0 where possible.
  5. Check your work and revise if necessary.

🖱️ Click or tap a grid intersection to add a point. Blue = your graph. Green dashed = correct graph (after reveal).
