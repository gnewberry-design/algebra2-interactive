📖 How to use the tools and construction hints

Compass: Click the center, then click a point to set the radius. This draws a full circle, representing the arc of a fixed-radius compass. You may use intersections as centers or radius points.

Straightedge: Click two points to draw an infinite straight line through them. You can select intersections of circles and lines.

Mark point: Click to place a helper point. Undo: Removes the last completed construction object. Clear: Removes all student work, but retains the given problem.

Parallel: One approach is to copy the angle made by a transversal using equal-radius compass circles.

Perpendicular: Make a circle centered at P that crosses the given line twice. From the two crossing points, draw equal-radius circles that intersect on the other side. Draw a line through P and their intersection.

Checking requires a final student-drawn straightedge line and at least two compass circles. The geometric check uses a tolerance to account for pointer placement; it checks the resulting line, not every classical construction step.
