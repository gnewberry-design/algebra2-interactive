📝 Exercise 1: Degree and Intercepts

1. What is the degree of f(x) = ax³ + bx² + cx + d, assuming a ≠ 0?

2. What is the maximum number of distinct x-intercepts a cubic polynomial can have?

🦸 Exercise 2: End Behavior

3. Move the a slider to a positive value. What happens to the ends of the graph?

4. Move the a slider to a negative value. How does the end behavior change?

5. Explain in your own words how changing the sign of a affects the cubic graph.

For a positive leading coefficient, the left end falls and the right end rises. For a negative leading coefficient, the left end rises and the right end falls. The sign reverses the end behavior.
