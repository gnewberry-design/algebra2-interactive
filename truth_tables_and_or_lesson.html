📖 KEY VOCABULARY

🔹 Statement: A sentence that is either TRUE or FALSE.

🔹 Truth Value: The classification TRUE (T) or FALSE (F).

🔹 Compound Statement: Two statements joined by a logical connector.

🟢 Conjunction: An AND statement represented by ∧.

🔵 Disjunction: An OR statement represented by ∨.

📊 Truth Table: A table showing every possible combination of truth values.

🔤 P and Q: Letters representing simple statements.

🟢 RULE #1: AND (∧)

P ∧ Q means P AND Q.

⭐ AND is TRUE ONLY when BOTH statements are TRUE! ⭐

📊 AND Truth Table

PQP ∧ Q
TT🟢 T
TF🔴 F
FT🔴 F
FF🔴 F

Memory Trick: 🟢 AND = ALL conditions must be TRUE.

🔵 RULE #2: OR (∨)

P ∨ Q means P OR Q.

⭐ OR is TRUE when AT LEAST ONE statement is TRUE! ⭐

📊 OR Truth Table

PQP ∨ Q
TT🟢 T
TF🟢 T
FT🟢 T
FF🔴 F

⚠️ Important: Mathematical OR is TRUE even when BOTH statements are TRUE.

Memory Trick: 🔵 OR = ONE OR MORE conditions must be TRUE.

👨‍🏫 I DO — TEACHER MODELING

💵🧥 Example 1: AND

P: I earned money today. 🟢 TRUE

Q: I bought a new jacket. 🔴 FALSE

Compound Statement: "I earned money today AND I bought a new jacket."

  1. Identify the connector: AND (∧).
  2. Determine P: TRUE.
  3. Determine Q: FALSE.
  4. Ask: Are BOTH statements true?
  5. No! Therefore, the compound statement is FALSE.
🔴 P ∧ Q = FALSE

🍕🌮 Example 2: OR

P: I ate pizza for lunch. 🔴 FALSE

Q: I ate tacos for lunch. 🟢 TRUE

Compound Statement: "I ate pizza for lunch OR I ate tacos for lunch."

  1. Identify the connector: OR (∨).
  2. Determine P: FALSE.
  3. Determine Q: TRUE.
  4. Ask: Is AT LEAST ONE statement true?
  5. Yes! Therefore, the compound statement is TRUE.
🟢 P ∨ Q = TRUE

💡 Teacher Think-Aloud

"I don't guess based on the story. I look at the truth values of P and Q, identify the connector, and apply the correct rule."

🚨 COMMON MISTAKES & RETEACHING

❌ Mistake: AND is true when only one part is true.

✅ Correction: AND requires BOTH parts to be true.

❌ Mistake: OR is false when both parts are true.

✅ Correction: OR is true when ONE OR BOTH parts are true.

❌ Mistake: Mixing up ∧ and ∨.

✅ Correction: Label the connector before evaluating the statement.

❌ Mistake: Guessing based on what seems realistic.

✅ Correction: Use the given truth values of P and Q.
